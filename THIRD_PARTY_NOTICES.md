# Dependencies and artwork

The browser bundle uses Three.js (MIT), Manifold (Apache-2.0), fflate (MIT), and d3-contour/d3-array/internmap (ISC). Their license texts are in `licenses/` and embedded in the standalone HTML. Version resolution is recorded in `package-lock.json`. Build/test tooling and additional installed dependencies retain their package licenses; node_modules is not distributed in this source archive.

`public/rocket.svg` and the contour test fixture originate from the user's rocket drawing, cleaned and decorated during this project using image generation and tracing. Other generated artwork from the conversation is not bundled in this release.

The central round symbol in `src/art/double-happiness.svg` is adapted from Wikimedia Commons' “Rounded symbol for shuangxi-U-1F264.svg,” dedicated to the public domain under CC0. The surrounding cloud ornament and final composition are original to this project. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rounded_symbol_for_shuangxi-U-1F264.svg

`src/tested-profile.json` preserves the project's independently constructed, physically tested 45 mm CAD channel. It is a tessellated snapshot of the parametric 16-lobe profile generated by the earlier Python/CadQuery implementation from diameter, scallop-depth and valley-radius settings. The linked MakerWorld model was used as visual inspiration and a rough dimensional reference; its downloadable geometry was not inspected or copied.
